What I found

01

Your homepage video is a 149 MB file.

The autoplaying video on your homepage is 149,198,538 bytes: 56 seconds at 1440 by 2560 and 50 frames per second. In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, the homepage transferred about 34 MB, its main content showed at 15.1 and 11.6 seconds, and the speed score was 61 and 64 out of 100. In the same test the Contact page scored 91 and Machine Rental 94. One test setup, two runs on the homepage; field speed is unmeasured.

02

Your homepage video shows a different phone number than your call button.

Your call button, footer and Contact page use 702-970-1188. The homepage video has “FREE ESTIMATES 702-886-7061” written into its picture, and several of your TikTok and Instagram videos show that same 886 number. Other videos show 702-970-1188. I didn't dial either number, so I can't say which one is current.

03

Your Instagram link doesn't open a profile.

The footer links to instagram.com/renovationspaintingepoxynmore. When I opened it without logging in, Instagram said “Profile isn't available”. The account that lists mancavespecialists.com as its website is @mancave_concrete_coatings_lv (486 followers when I looked), and your site doesn't link to it. A logged-in view may differ from what I saw.

04

Your contact email is on a domain that says it's under construction.

Every page lists sales@renovationspaintingepoxy.com. When I opened renovationspaintingepoxy.com it showed “MANCAVE SPECIALISTS is under construction”. A search for your name also returned that page as its third result. I didn't send an email, so I can't say whether messages to that address arrive.

05

Your services have no pages of their own.

Your sitemap lists six pages: Home, About, Before & After, Gallery, Machine Rental and Contact. Epoxy, metallic finishes, polished concrete, house painting, commercial painting and pressure washing each get a card or a line, not a page. In four searches for what you do (garage epoxy, metallic epoxy, polished concrete, machine rental), your site was not among the results, and other Las Vegas companies were named. Only a search for your name found you.

06

Nothing on the site lets a visitor check you out.

I searched the text of all six pages for a customer review, “license”, “insured”, “bonded” and “warranty”. None appears, and no page links to a Google or Yelp listing. I can't tell whether you have them; the pages just don't say. Your before and after photos and your line “We fix bad work done by other companies” are the main proof a visitor gets.

What's working

Every page has its own title and description, a canonical address and alt text on every image. The accessibility and SEO scores were 100 on every page I tested, and best practices was 100 on all but Machine Rental (96). Your phone is a tap-to-call link on every page. The Contact page has a short form (name, phone, email, service, details), the Machine Rental page states a daily price range, and the Before & After and Gallery pages show the work. Your robots.txt doesn't block AI crawlers, and you link your TikTok.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks on a phone (untested), whether the contact form or the email address reach you, whether you have a Google listing, and what the Instagram profile shows when logged in. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt allows every crawler and lists your sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML contains about 750 words and one clear heading (“Premium Epoxy Flooring”), so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Found
    Title and description on every page

    All six pages I opened have their own title and description, a canonical address and a page heading.

  • Found
    Phone is a tap-to-call link

    Every page links your phone as tel:7029701188 and shows 702-970-1188 as text.

  • Gap
    No business markup

    The homepage has no structured data. Search tools can't read your name, phone, service area or business type as data. They only get them from the page text.

  • Gap
    Instagram link doesn't open a profile

    The footer link to instagram.com/renovationspaintingepoxynmore showed “Profile isn't available” when I opened it without logging in. The account that lists mancavespecialists.com is @mancave_concrete_coatings_lv, and no page links to it.

  • Note
    No street address on the site

    No page shows a street address, which fits a mobile business. It also means there is no map or Google listing link to follow. I couldn't tell whether you have a Google listing.

  • Note
    Sitemap format address

    Your sitemap.xml lists six pages. Its format line is written as https://www.sitemaps.org/schemas/sitemap/0.9, while the published standard uses http://. I can't say whether search engines accept it as written.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
